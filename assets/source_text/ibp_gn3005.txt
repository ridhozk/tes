   1. PURPOSE

       This guideline described the Integrated Business Planning ("IBP”) process, a collaborative
       approach to strategic planning. It describes the purpose and process in each phase of the cycle
       including the roles and responsibilities (RACI) of each function who are involved in each phase
       of the cycle.

       The IBP process ensures all aspects of the business are considered, applying Plan-Do-Assess-
       Adjust (“PDAA”) approach enabling continuous improvement.

   2. SCOPE

       This guideline applies to all MedcoEnergi’s E&P Assets (Operated and Non-Operated). This
       describes the planning process (at the minimum) that is required to be done in asset level in
       the context of Long-Term Planning (“LTP”) process until the process that happens in E&P
       Planning’s desk. E&P Planning consolidates overall E&P Assets’ planning, assessing the
       projects in alignment with Company’s business objectives and deliver the result to Corporate
       Planning and PT. Medco Energi International (“MEI”) Board of Directors (“BOD”).

       This guideline excludes the process that happens in the MEI Corporate Planning. However,
       this guideline is developed in alignment with the business process there.

   3. RESPONSIBILITIES

       This guideline is owned by E&P Planning who owns the overall IBP process. E&P Planning
       drives and monitor the implementation of the process within overall E&P Assets in collaboration
       with each Asset Management, Business Analyst, and other Functions who has role in the LTP
       process. In alignment with the PDAA approach, the process described in the document is to be
       regularly reviewed (at least once a year) and adjusted if necessary.

       With the current Asset based organization concept in Medco E&P, the implementation of this
       process in each asset is the accountability of each Asset’s SVP and Senior Manager, General
       Manager (for NOA and International) supported by Asset Planning Manager(s) under E&P
       Planning organization.

       While E&P Planning is accountable to deliver a robust Long-Term Plan for overall E&P Portfolio
       through this IBP process to MedcoEnergi Corporate Planning function for further consolidation
       with other MedcoEnergi business line.

4.1.2   Field Depletion Plan (“FDP”)

        FDP provides robust and integrated technical work for Asset Development Plan. FDP outlines
        the strategy for the management and extraction of hydrocarbon reserves from an oil & gas field
        over its lifecycle. It includes detailed projections of production rates, reservoir behaviour, well
        performance, and depletion strategies. The FDP aims to optimize production while ensuring
        the efficient utilization of resources and maximizing the economic value of the field. Please refer
        to FDP Guideline Document No. GL-001/06.2021/MEDC/RESV-TA as the governance of the
        FDP process.

4.1.3   Asset Development Plan (“ADP”)

        ADP focuses on the long-term development of oil and gas asset. It involves strategic planning
        for the exploration, appraisal, development and production phases of various assets. The ADP
        integrates the technical, optimize resource allocation, commercial and financial considerations
        to prioritize investment decision within an asset. Please refer to ADP Guideline Document No.
        GL-002/06.2021/MEDC/RESV-TA as the governance of the ADP process.

4.1.4   Long Term Plan (“LTP”)

        The LTP is built based on the ADP from each Assets which undergo exercise and discussion
        in E&P level during portfolio evaluation. The LTP not only listing down work programs, but it is
        built based on robust assumptions (short until long term) of commercial view, subsurface
        opportunities, development plan, field’s operating mode, including manpower assumptions in
        10-Year window. This is an important document which enables the reader to see where the
        Company is heading. Therefore, it is critical to have a robust assumption and credible scenario
        as well as numbers in order to enable Management to make a well-informed decision.

        During the LTP exercise, E&P Planning receives guideline from Corporate Planning that
        outlines any boundaries that we need to comply with such as, but not limited to:

           Capex limitation for the first two years in Scenario 1 & 2,
           Exchange Currency rate & Oil price to be used,
           Unit Cost/barrel that we need to maintain,
           All producing Asset expect to have positive Cash Flows

4.2.1   Scenario Definition

        Scenario Definition is the basis for all disciplines to work in this LTP exercise. This is required
        in order to have the same understanding in generating the required profiles. Please refer to
        RACI table, Step 2.1 for developing Scenario Definition. In this phase, assumptions need to be
        established such as commercial assumptions, operating mode, DOE assumptions.

        A Scenario is a package of projects designed to be implemented in a specific Block within a
        specific period. In a Scenario, and Activity or Program must be part of a Project. A Project in a
        Scenario must be defined as Firm, Future or Upside Project. An Activity is a specific job in a
        specific Block/field/well. A program is a group of Activities with similar Program Type. An Activity
        or Program must be registered in Hopper List. This way the Subsurface drive is established in
        this LTP process. All subsurface initiatives which are currently stored in Petroleum Technical
        Data Management ”PTDM” correlates with Hopper ID, of which connects with IBP business
        process.

        In this IBP process, since we are utilizing Database, so naming convention is crucial to be
        followed.

        Scenario naming convention:
        <BLOCK>-<PLANNING TYPE>-<YEAR>-<TITLE>-(<PLANNING CYCLE>)
        <PLANNING TYPE> : ADP, LTP, UPDATED LTP, etc
        <TITLE>: a short description of the scenario contents
        <BLOCK>,<PLANNING TYPE>,<YEAR> are chosen from drop down list
        <PLANNING CYCLE> is automatic generated by system

4.2.2   Profile Generation

        For each production and cost profile, as long as an agreed set of scenario definition has been
        established, then the two profile can be generated in parallel. If a profile (either cost or
        production) requires a change in activity or program, then the scenario must be changed before
        carrying out further processing. Below is the naming convention for Production/Cost Profile
        Version:

        Production/Cost profile version naming convention:
        <SCENARIO NAME>-<TYPE>-<SEQUENCE NUMBER>-(<PLANNING CYCLE>)
        <TYPE>: PP for production profile, CP for cost profile
        <SCENARIO NAME> and <TYPE> are chosen from drop down list
        <SEQUENCE NUMBER> and <PLANNING CYCLE> is automatic generated by system



4.2.3   Data Review Process
4.2.3.1 Integrated Review

        After the profiles are generated, prior to upload process to the IBP Data Input Application
        (Please refer to Section 4.3 regarding Tools), it is expected that each Data Owner to perform
        review for each set of data. In this process, it is also expected that Asset Management together
        with BA, Asset’s Planning Manager and Investment Appraisal Analyst of each asset to perform
        integrated review to making sense of the data since this process involves large data set and it
        is crucial to have integrated team review and discussion.

4.2.3.2 Pairing, Release & Lock Data

        Following the integrated review, the uploaded data needs to be paired between the Cost profile
        and the Production Profile in the IBP Application. Please refer to RACI Table Section 7.4 & 7.5
        for Pairing, Release and Lock Data.

4.2.4   Economic Evaluation

        The locked data will be evaluated by Investment Appraisal team to determine if the project is
        economic as per Company’s threshold. Every year, the threshold for project economics will be
        communicated through Corporate LTP Guideline (Refer to section 4.1.4) It is potential to have
        iteration in this step where if it is found that a project is not economic, the data input will likely
        to change.

           If all project layers are already Ok, Investment appraisal team will evaluate layer by layer
           valuation to obtain cashflow for every project and also asset level for each Firm, Future and
           Upside layer. The cashflow numbers will be later uploaded to IBP Consolidation tool to complete
           the Asset Data set for Consolidation process.

4.2.5      Consolidation Process

           Consolidation is to be performed when all Assets have complete data sets up to cashflow. This
           process happens in IBP Consolidation Tool in BPM. Basically, consolidation is to have an
           overall E&P view of driver numbers and PSC Cashflow.

           For Full Package of LTP, Corporate Planning requires E&P Planning to submit not only up to
           PSC Cashflow, but also requires GAAP Financial of which requiring collaboration with E&P
           Financial Reporting group and also Reserve Management. This is being worked on outside the
           currently available IBP Tool in BPM. However, Corporate Planning issues template for the team
           to use and being updated every year.

4.2.6      Green House Gas Emission & Reserves

           Part of LTP Package, Green House Gas (“GHG”) Emission is also part of the discussion where
           the Company needs to understand where we are against our ESG Commitment. For this
           purpose, Corporate HSE will work with HSE Team in each Asset to produce GHG Profile for
           E&P considering the relevant Asset Scenario and Assumptions. This is being worked on outside
           the currently available IBP Tool in BPM. However, Corporate Planning issues template for the
           team to use and being updated every year.

           For reserve, Reserve Management Team is in communication with Asset Management and
           Planning to calculate the net reserve addition, future reserve booking in order to be able to
           calculate the GAAP Financial Statement. Please refer to Section 8.2 in the RACI Table.

4.2.7      Submission and Review with Board of Directors (“BOD”)

           Refer to Figure 2 of LTP Business Process, there is a set schedule for package submission to
           Corporate Planning and reviews with BOD. Normally there are iterations following the reviews
           with feedbacks given from the BOD. After the package is approved, the package is then
           consolidated in Corporate level to be brought to BOD & BOC Meeting for the following year
           Budget approval process.

        takes place. E&P Planning serves as facilitator throughout the process. A RACI Table had been
        prepared to serve as basis for the collaboration in this Integrated Business Planning process.
