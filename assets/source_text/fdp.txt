1. Purpose and Scope

   This document provides a process to ensure regular cross functional reviews of the business unit
   depletion plan (FDP), based on technical evaluation or assessment by field. FDP provides robust
   and integrated technical work for Asset Development Plan.

   The focus of FDP is at field level where it integrates technical & functional considerations for an
   asset as a basis to deliver short-term business delivery and growth

   The FDP will be updated every year, even though the overall reservoir management strategies will
   remain unchanged. The updates need to incorporate the latest team’s evaluation on the field and
   the latest plan to optimize hydrocarbon production and maximize recovery in the most technically
   and cost effective way.

   Scope of the depletion plan document should include but not limited to:

           Asset overview
           Operational strategy of asset
           Base production review including existing wells and facilities
           Subsurface management plan, including reservoir characterization, fluid types, drive
            mechanism, injection/production levels, number of wells, and a surveillance plan.
           Any future capital investments either in well or facility.

   The document shall cover all active fields in each asset, and must be approved by Senior Manager
   Subsurface.

3. Guidelines
   3.1 Timing of Depletion Plan Review

   Field Depletion Plan review should be conducted once each year to reflect the latest business
   environment and field performance. The review process is recommended to start commencing in
   early first quarter (Q1) of the year. This shall capture any changes in term of base performance,
   wellwork and workover, new wells project and updated reservoir management plan to as reference
   for asset depletion scenario.

   Figure below depict the timing of overall Business Planning Analysis.Field Depletion Plan should
   be preceded by updating reservoir characterization and understanding, well and facility review and
   opportunity generation, where it started in Q1-Q2.

   review. After that, series of review with Technical Assurance should be performed for example
   major well and workover and base decline review. FDP production data should be base on 2+10

   3.2 Planning a Depletion Plan Review

   Depletion plan document review shall be initiated by Sr. Manager Subsurface. The initiator will call
   for a kick-off meeting typically inviting following functions:

       a. Base Management Manager
       b. Resource Development and/or Reservoir Modelling Manager
       c.   Operation Engineering Manager
       d. Operation Manager
       e. Sr. Manager Surface Engineering
       f.   Sr. Manager Well Operations
       g. Facility Engineering Manager
       h. Asset Management Manager



   During planning and preparation, Sr. Manager Subsurface is responsible for integration to ensure
   all parties are well informed that Depletion Plan Review process has been started, ensure they
   understand the objective of the document review, establish a timeline to complete the review that
   are agreed by all parties. The kick-off meeting shall also assign a Coordinator who will closely
   monitor and track the progress, compile the updates from each function to be in single document,
   as well as ensure the approval process. The kick-off meeting is recommended to commence in
   early first quarter of the year so that the result is included in the Long Term Plan.

4. Topics to cover

   The document shall cover the following sections:

       1. Introduction
            This section shall simply explain document objective and why depletion plan is required.
       2. Executive Summary
            Explain summary of this field depletion plan and major changes as to compare to previous
            year assessment.
       3. Asset Description
            This section informs overview of the assets, location map, historical fields discovery and
            development, existing Plans of Development (POD) of assets approved by Government of
            Indonesia, working interest, block expiricy, structural geology, mapping and modelling,
            reservoirs (include drive mechanism and fluid properties), production history and
            forecast,existing process facility, existing well and field operation, well integrity, and/or
            available gas contracts.

     4. Field Depletion Plan
          Depletion plan for individual active field shall be clearly described and contains the following
          information but not limited to:
          -   Asset overview, which informs:
                  o    When the asset was started to be developed
                  o    Subsurface formations being developed
          -   Operational strategy of asset, which informs general information about:
                  o    Equipment performance monitoring and maintenance
                  o    Current ongoing project for facility installation
                  o    Any defined future capital investments in facility
                  o    Historical DOE
                  o    Fuel optimization or reduction effort
          -   Surface facilities, which describes:
                  o    Location of facilities
                  o    General process and utility flow diagram of the facilities
                  o    Available system capacityof the existing facility to manage the additional fluid
                  o    Include to review possibility for facility sharing agreement with other asset/
