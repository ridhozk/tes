1. Purpose and Scope

   This document provides a process to ensure regular cross-functional reviews of the Asset
   Development Plan, based on Field Depletion Plan. ADP provides live and robust product for LTP
   input and clear articulation of value for each scenarios.

   The focus of ADP is at Block or area level - Bigger picture, focus on strategy decision (including the
   economics sensitivity, and commercial options).

   ADP will be updated every year, unless there is no significant changes in the asset strategy or asset
   value.

   Scope of the depletion plan document should include but not limited to:

           Area strategy (incl GSA) and overview,
           Resource base,
           Resource development options,
           Risk management plan,
           5 year plan,
           Production profiles and DOE
           Field life assessment
           HSE: policy and facility integrity
           Cost: operation, optimization
           Technology plan,
           Exploration strategy

   The document shall cover all active Block and must be approved by Medco EP President Director.

A           Accountable - The person who is ultimately accountable for results. He / She has yes / no
            authority or veto power. Only one "A" can be assigned to a function of activity.

C            Counsult - A person to be consulted prior to a final decision of action, 2-way communication.

I            Inform - A person who must be informed after the final decision or action is taken. 1-way
