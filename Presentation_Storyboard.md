## MEPG Integrated Planning & Scheduling Guideline — Rev 3 → Rev 4 Executive Update

**Deliverable:** `Integrated_Planning_Guideline_Rev4_Executive_Update.pptx` (17 slides: 8 core + appendix divider + 8 appendix)
**Audience:** SM / SVP — has NOT read either guideline. Goal: endorsement of Rev 4 direction.
**Duration:** 10–15 min. Style: MBB (Pyramid Principle, MECE, ONE message per slide, minimal text, strong visuals).
**Built into:** the user's branded `skeleton_deck.pptx` — masters, layouts, logos, theme and brand blues (`004C8F` title / `0070C0` banner) preserved. Content area is white with the corner logo from the master.

**Design system (consistent across deck):**
- **ONE message per slide** carried in the full-width blue headline banner (the "so-what").
- **Motif:** rounded-rectangle content cards + navy value/impact strips; blue `0070C0` banner; amber `FFC000` diamonds used *only* for stage-gates so Gate 1/2/3 read instantly.
- **Colour progression** (strategic → operational) mirrors source Figure 2: navy `004C8F` → blue `1F6FB2`/`2E86C1` → dark slate `26324A`. Green `548235` marks "confirmed in practice" evidence.
- **Fonts:** brand `Univers 67/57 Condensed` on titles/banners (matches skeleton); `Calibri` for all new body/table text (QA-safe, ships with Office).
- **Brand-hex caveat:** exact MedcoEnergi brand hex codes are **unconfirmed**; the deck reuses the skeleton's own blues as placeholders and this is noted on the Source Register slide. Swap in official hex before external use.
- No accent stripes / colour bars (per pptx skill); separation via tint, whitespace, and the navy strips.
